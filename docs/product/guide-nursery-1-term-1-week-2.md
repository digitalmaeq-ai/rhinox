# Guide: "My Day Has Order" (Nursery 1, Term 1, Week 2)

**DRAFT. The on-screen part is not released** until a curriculum reviewer approves it (see the review notes at the end). The lesson itself needs no screen and can be taught from the Teacher Guide (pp. 9–10) today. About 40 minutes with the book; the screen part is about 10 minutes.

## What your child learns
To put the morning in order: wake up, get dressed, walk to school. Words: morning, routine, order, before, after.

## What you need
Three morning cards per group of 4 (wake up, get dressed, walk to school), a taped floor grid, cardboard Ayo (optional), Activity Book and crayons. No device or kit is required.

## The steps
1. **Think.** "Silly Ayo walked to school before he got dressed! What must come first?" Let 3–4 children tell their morning.
2. **Move.** Act it out three times: stretch, pull on a shirt, march on the spot.
3. **Code.** Groups of 4: one lays the three cards in a row by the floor grid, two act them as Ayo, one checks the order. Say "Swap!" and change roles.
4. **Build.** Optional this week.
5. **Create / Activity Book.** Number the three morning pictures 1, 2, 3, then draw your morning in three boxes: first, next, last. Not marked.
6. **Check (teacher).** Watch the child put three routine pictures in order and act them out.

## On the screen (when released)
The child taps three picture cards in order (3 tries). The app only checks the order on the screen. It cannot see acting or drawing, and it never says "passed". The official check is you watching.

## Adults: do and avoid
- Ask "What comes before walk to school?" Let the child answer.
- Do not place the cards for the child, and do not record the check unless you watched.

## If your child is stuck
Start with two cards, then add the third. Act it out first, then lay the cards.

## Privacy
Nothing is saved. Do not type a child's name into any page.

## Review notes for the book team (found while comparing the Activity Book and Teacher Guide)
1. Activity Book p. 8 captions say "Emeka wakes up / puts on his uniform / walks to school", but the lesson, video and workbook use **Ayo**. Choose one name.
2. Card wording differs between books: Teacher Guide cards say "wake up, get dressed, walk to school"; Activity Book p. 10 says "waking up, putting on his shirt, walking to school". The screen uses the Teacher Guide wording ("Wake up", "Get dressed", "Walk to school").
3. The Activity Book "Number my morning" pictures are shuffled (walking, waking, shirt). That matches the activity; keep it.
