## Order of work
1. Week 3 approved and launched (proves the launch contract).
2. Prediction mode (Week 4), then debug mode (Week 6). Weeks 7–10 reuse them.
3. Two-stop route (Week 8). Week 11 map is unplugged first.
4. Unplugged weeks (1, 2, 5, 12, 13): guides and teacher checklists, using the existing ordering exercise where useful.
5. Polish phase (decision 12): downloads, workbooks, badges, QR codes and links, then Term 1 becomes the template.

## Rules that apply to every week
- The app never marks understanding as passed. The official check is the workbook's teacher observation.
- Each week needs: curriculum record, reviewed spec, guide, review sheet. Developers do not mark specs reviewed.
- No QR code or badge points to a page that is not reviewed and live.
