# Guide: "I Can Do Things in Steps" (Nursery 1, Term 1, Week 1)

For teachers and parents. About 10 minutes. This guide describes only what the Code Studio activity does today.

## What your child learns
To follow three steps in the right order: wake up, brush teeth, eat breakfast. The words are step, first, next, last and order.

## Before you start
- You need a phone, tablet or computer with the page open. The page works without a robot or any kit.
- No device? Use three printed or hand-drawn cards (wake up, brush teeth, eat breakfast) and do the same steps on a table. This is the official no-device route.
- Sit with your child. This activity is made to be done with a grown-up.

## The steps on the screen
1. **Think.** Look at the three pictures together. Ask: what happens first? next? last?
2. **Move.** Play "Robot Says": clap, stamp, turn. Then change the order. Tap the button when you have tried it. The app cannot see this, so it trusts you.
3. **Code.** Your child taps the pictures in order, then taps "Check my order". They have 3 tries.
4. **Build.** Optional this week. You can skip it.
5. **Create.** Your child draws three steps of something they do every day (or arranges three objects). Tap the button when done. The app cannot see the drawing.
6. **Show a grown-up what you can do.** You watch your child follow a 3-step instruction on their own, then tell the page what you saw.

## What the adult should do
- Read the instructions aloud. Let your child tap the pictures.
- Say each step slowly, pause, then say "Go!"
- Praise waiting and listening.

## What the adult should avoid
- Do not tap the answer for your child.
- Do not press "Yes, I watched them do it" unless you really watched them follow three steps on their own.

## If your child gets it wrong
- Use the hint. Start with two steps, then add the third.
- After 3 tries the page shows the right order. Look at it together, do the steps with your child, then tap "A grown-up helped me finish". This is saved as **completed with help**. It is not a pass, and that is fine. Try the cards again on another day.

## If you cannot watch right now
Tap "I will check later". The page will say a teacher check is still to do. It does not count as confirmed.

## What the final screen means
- **Activity complete:** your child ordered the pictures on their own and an adult said they watched.
- **Finished with help:** the ordering needed a grown-up's help.
- **Waiting for teacher check:** the observation has not happened yet.
- The "extra practice" question is only practice. It never replaces the adult check.

## Saving and privacy
Nothing is saved to an account. Progress lasts only until you close or refresh the page. Do not type your child's name or details into the reflection box.
